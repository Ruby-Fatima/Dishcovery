DISHCOVERY RECIPE

Shakshuka

A delicious, comforting dish made with eggs, tomatoes and warm spices.

PREP TIME

10 min

COOK TIME

20 min

SERVINGS

2 people

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 ripe tomatoes
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika

How to make it

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the chopped onion.
  2. Add tomatoes and spices. Cook until the sauce thickens.
  3. Make small spaces in the sauce and add the eggs.
  4. Cover and cook until the eggs are done to your liking.
